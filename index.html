Good to know

Questions, answered.

Why aren't the prices listed on this page?

Because the honest price depends on where you're staying, how many of you there are and what you want to do. Message us with those three things and you'll have a fixed, all-in number in minutes — usually faster than reading a price table.

How long is a day tour?

Eight to nine hours door to door, from IDR 900k for the car. If you want an earlier start, a sunset stop or a longer day, extra hours can be added when you book.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Simple: if you genuinely didn't enjoy your day with us, tell your driver before the tour ends and you don't pay for it. No forms, no arguing. We only ask that you tell us on the day, so we can try to fix it while you're still with us. It covers our own service — the car, the driver, the planning — not third-party tickets or activities we booked for you.

How do I pay?

Cash in rupiah or bank transfer, on the day. Nothing to pay when you book.

We're a big group — can you take us?

Our own car seats 6 comfortably. From 7 people up we arrange a bigger vehicle, or a second car and driver, and organise the whole trip as one group with one price. Tell us exactly how many you are and we'll work it out.

Do you pick up at the airport at night?

Yes, any hour. We track your flight, so a delay is never a problem, and your driver waits inside with a sign with your name on it.
