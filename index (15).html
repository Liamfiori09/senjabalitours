Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I pay?

Cash (IDR) or card on the day. For tours we may ask a small deposit via bank transfer or Wise to secure your date.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes — free cancellation or changes up to 24 hours before pickup. Rain plan? We'll help you reshuffle the itinerary at no cost.

Are entrance fees included in tours?

No — tour prices cover the private car, driver, fuel and parking for the full day. We publish the exact ticket price of every stop before you book so you can budget precisely.

We're a big group — can you take us?

Our own car seats 6 comfortably. For groups up to 13 we run tours with a larger vehicle at a group rate — message us for a quote.

Do you do airport pickups at night?

Yes, 24/7. Pickups between 10pm and 6am carry a small IDR 100k supplement. We track your flight, so delays are never a problem.

How does the satisfaction guarantee work?

Simple: if you genuinely didn't enjoy your tour or transfer, tell us before the day ends and we refund you 100%. We'd rather lose one fare than one reputation.
